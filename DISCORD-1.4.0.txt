@everyone Porthole 1.4.0 "All Hands on Deck" is out ⚓   (DRAFT: not posted; the download links are filled in at release time)

The headline: **Discord, inside Porthole.** Plus a rebuilt in-game menu, much easier controls, and interactive tutorials.

**What's new in 1.4.0**
• **Discord (experimental, off until you turn it on):** servers, channels, DMs and forums in a new Social tab; voice calls with your game's sound in the call; banners over the game; "playing on Porthole" on your profile; and a viewer for Go Live streams
• **A rebuilt in-game menu:** fills a phone's screen, see-through so the game shows behind it, and a resizable floating card on iPad
• **Controls made easy:** pick how every game is controlled once, starter layouts, a Universal default, a second on-screen pad layout, left-handed mode, and an editor that works on several controls at once
• **Test your controls:** a 3D DualSense, DualShock 4 or Xbox pad that moves with the real one, plus keyboard and mouse tests and a stick deadzone
• **Tutorials** that point at the real buttons, and a first-run setup where you choose how you play
• **TV or monitor on a cable:** the game fills that screen, the controls stay on your device
• **Steam:** games on an external drive are found by themselves, and an update only asks for the space it really needs

⚠️ **About Discord:** Porthole is not an official Discord client. Discord's terms do not allow third-party clients, and an account used with one can be limited or banned. Use an account you can afford to lose. Porthole tells Discord what it is.

Compatibility badges start again for this version, so every game shows "Not yet tested" until someone has played it. A report after you play helps everyone.

**💬 Discord**
• **Sign in** on discord.com's own page or by scanning a code with the Discord app. Porthole never sees your password; only the sign-in token is kept, in the Keychain
• **Text:** channels, DMs and group chats, forums and threads; replies, reactions, editing and deleting your own messages, typing indicators, link cards, stickers, a picture viewer
• **Voice:** join a channel, deafen, microphone sensitivity, a volume and a mute for each person, push to talk. Your game's sound can go into the call
• **Over the game:** banners for messages and calls with Join and Decline on them
• **Go Live:** a LIVE mark on people streaming, and a viewer for their streams (experimental)
• **Closing the app** from the app switcher leaves your call and shows you offline
• One switch turns all of it on or off, and it is off until you do

**🎮 Controls and the menu**
• **Settings > Controls > Default controls:** choose once; starter layouts from a picture of each (Universal, Gamepad, Action, Point and click, Platformer)
• **Universal** is the new default for most games; the on-screen controller has a Corner sticks layout; on iPad the picture moves above the controls
• **A controls card for each game** and a quick layout switch in the game
• **The in-game menu** is see-through and fills a phone; on iPad it is a card you can drag to any size
• **Input lag and Screen delay** numbers for the performance overlay

**🧭 Also**
• Tutorials and a longer first-run setup; a Community and links page; Steam download region; Epic games start the way Epic's launcher starts them; Steam Cloud saves Steam sends packed in other ways now download

Something wrong? **Settings > Report a problem** sends the log (cleaned of names, e-mail and public IPs; you can read it first). Thanks for testing 🙏
