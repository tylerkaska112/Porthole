📝 **Game reports: what's changing and why**

When you report how a game runs, Porthole now sends that game's log along with it.

**Why:** "doesn't work" on its own doesn't tell me what went wrong, and a game shouldn't be listed as broken without a reason. With the log I can see what actually crashed, fix it, and tell a real game problem from a setup problem.

**How it works**
• The log is private. It is never shown in Reports and only I can read it.
• It's cleaned first: personal paths, account names, e-mail addresses and public IPs are taken out. The report screen shows you the log's size before you send.
• Your name and accounts are never sent.
• Every report is looked at before it appears in Reports or counts toward a game's badge, so give it a little time. My reports tells you when one is waiting.
