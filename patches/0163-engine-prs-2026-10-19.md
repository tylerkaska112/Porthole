## Ours, same patch: 0163 steering windows (`virtual_ios.c`)

`ios_steer_window_usable`: the steering valve's windows (`[0x7400000000, ...)` and the top slot) are skipped when they start at or above `host_addr_space_limit`. Without the extended-virtual-addressing entitlement that limit is 32 GB, `allocate_virtual_memory` clamped the window's end to it and `map_free_area` then logged `[va-scan] ml749 INVERTED WINDOW` twice for every refused large reserve, and asked the kernel for nothing. **This does not make a game that needs more address space start**: a Unity game (Aeons Echo, 2026-10-19 reports) still cannot place its 511 MB reservation in the 17 GB the kernel gives such an install; the placement walk (`ios_ml1027_place`) finds a 1862 MB gap and `anon_mmap_tryfixed` fails in it eight times, which is not understood yet.
