3D model of the DualSense controller
====================================

The 3D controller shown in Settings > Controls > Test controller is based on the model

    "Playstation 5 Dualsense" by AHarmlessPotato
    https://skfb.ly/opKOx

licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0):
http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/   (legal code: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode)

Changes made by Porthole: the model was turned to Porthole's axes and centred, split into the parts that move in the app (the sticks, the face buttons, the D-pad keys, the bumpers, the
triggers, the touchpad and the small buttons), each part was given a pivot, the parts that do not move were joined, and the model was converted to USD with its textures kept as separate
files. The textures are the model's own. The conversion script is tools/dualsense/prepare_dualsense.py in Porthole's source.

to picture a controller that is being tested.
